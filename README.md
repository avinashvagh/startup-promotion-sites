## 💡 Pro Tips

**Directories**
Submit to free, high Domain Rank sites first. Don't pay until you've exhausted all free options above DR 70.

**Reddit**
Never paste your link directly. Answer the question first. Mention your tool only when it's genuinely the best solution.

**Content**
Wait 1–2 weeks after publishing on your own blog before crossposting. Always add a canonical link back to your site to protect SEO.

**YouTube**
One demo video + OpusClip for shorts = 5x content from 1 recording. See the full workflow in file 08.

**Launch timing**
Don't launch everywhere on the same day. Stagger over 3–4 weeks to maintain momentum and get repeated traffic spikes.
