# ✍️ Writing & Content Publishing Platforms

Republish your blogs, articles, and technical content on these platforms
to reach new audiences, build authority, and get backlinks.
✅ = Free | 💰 = Paid

---

> 💡 **Pro tip:** Don't publish everything at once. Wait 1–2 weeks after your
> original post goes live, then crosspost with a canonical link back to your site.
> This protects your SEO while maximizing reach.

> 🔁 **Suggested Crossposting Order:**
> 1. Publish on your **own blog** first
> 2. Wait **1–2 weeks**
> 3. Crosspost to **Dev.to / Hashnode** (add canonical URL)
> 4. Republish on **Medium** via Import tool (preserves canonical)
> 5. Share on **HackerNoon** for tech audience
> 6. Post summary + link on **LinkedIn Articles**
> 7. Submit to **Hacker News** as Show HN
